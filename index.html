Weight guide

LIGHTYou can do 15 clean reps before it starts to feel hard. LIGHT–MEDIUM12–15 clean reps before it gets hard. MEDIUM10–12 clean reps before it gets hard. Form stays perfect. HEAVY6–8 good reps. Only for the main lifts in weeks 9–11 (Medium–Heavy).
Each exercise shows its kg range for Light, Medium and Heavy, with today's level highlighted. Machines differ between gyms, so treat the kg as a starting guide; the reps decide. Dumbbell weights are per hand.
Next step up: machines and cables +5 kg (pulley cables +2.5 kg), leg press +5 kg, dumbbells +2 kg per hand (the next dumbbell on the rack).
How the weights go up
  • Each exercise has a rep range, for example 8–10. Start at a weight that lets you reach the bottom of the range.
  • When you hit the top of the range on every set with good form, add one step next session (the amount shown under each exercise).
  • The new weight drops you back to the bottom of the range. Build up again.
  • Stuck for 3 sessions on one lift? Drop the weight 10% and climb again.
  • Every session: 5 min warm-up (upright bike, easy) + 10 box squats + 10 arm circles; 3 min cool-down and stretch at the end.
