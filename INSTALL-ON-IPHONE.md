# Round 3 on your iPhone — no Apple ID, no sign-in, free

The app has to be opened from a web address **once**. After you add it to your home screen, it opens from its own icon, full screen, and works **offline**.
Your data is saved **only on your iPhone**. Nobody else can see it, even someone who opens the same address.

## 1. Put the files online (once, about 10 minutes, free)
You can do this in Safari on the iPhone, or on any computer.

1. Go to **github.com** and create a free account.
2. Tap **+** → **New repository**. Name it `round3`, choose **Public**, tick **Add a README**, then tap **Create repository**.
   *(Public only means the empty app can be seen. Your data is never uploaded; it stays on your phone.)*
3. Tap **Add file** → **Upload files**, and select the 6 app files from the unzipped **Round3-WebApp** folder:
   `index.html`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`.
   Tap **Commit changes**.
4. Open **Settings** → **Pages**. Under *Branch* choose **main** and **/ (root)**, then tap **Save**.
5. Wait 1–2 minutes. The page then shows your address, like `https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/round3/`.

## 2. Add it to the home screen
1. Open that address in **Safari** on the iPhone.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Open **Round 3** from the new icon. From now on, always open it from the icon.

## Good to know
- **Back up now and then:** Today tab → *Your data* → **Back up my data** → save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. Removing the icon from the home screen deletes the app's data; **Restore a backup** brings it back.
- **No automatic Apple Watch data** in this version. Type in steps, Move, exercise minutes and weight from the Fitness app. It takes about 30 seconds a day.
- **Photos:** tap **Add a photo** on any exercise to save a picture from your photo library. Photos stay on the phone.
- **Updates:** when Claude sends you a new version, upload the new `index.html` to the same GitHub repository (Add file → Upload files; it replaces the old one). Open the app twice and it updates. Your data stays.
